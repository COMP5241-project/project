# Functional Requirements

## 1. Purpose and Scope

The platform shall provide mini-courses and learning activities in software engineering and software development. It shall support learners with different backgrounds and goals, instructors who deliver and adapt courses, and user-generated learning paths and mini-courses in other subject areas.

## 2. User Roles

- **Learner:** discovers and completes mini-courses and activities, and may create a self-directed learning path.
- **Instructor:** creates and adapts mini-courses, manages course participants, and monitors learner participation and engagement.

## 3. Functional Requirements

### 3.1 Course Topics and Content

- **FR-01:** The platform shall provide learning content covering the fundamentals of software engineering and software development.
- **FR-02:** The platform shall provide learning content on the effective use of AI coding agents in software development.
- **FR-03:** The platform shall provide learning content on soft skills and product skills used in software development.
- **FR-04:** The platform shall allow mini-courses to cover subjects or domains beyond software engineering and software development.

### 3.2 Learning Formats and Activities

- **FR-05:** The platform shall support learning content presented as text and images.
- **FR-06:** The platform shall support interactive simulations.
- **FR-07:** The platform shall support interactive role-play activities, including mock interviews.
- **FR-08:** The platform shall support games and mini-games as learning activities.
- **FR-09:** The platform shall support animations narrated by AI.
- **FR-10:** The platform shall support slide-based learning content.
- **FR-11:** The platform shall support online coding activities.

### 3.3 Personalized Learning

- **FR-12:** The platform shall allow learning content and activities to be tailored to learners' backgrounds and learning goals.
- **FR-13:** The platform shall provide learning experiences accessible to non-Computer Science majors who want to understand software development and software engineering.
- **FR-14:** The platform shall provide learning experiences relevant to Computer Science graduates preparing for careers in related fields in the age of AI.
- **FR-15:** The platform shall allow learners to specify their own topics and generate a self-directed learning path for those topics.

### 3.4 Instructor Course Authoring and Management

- **FR-16:** The platform shall allow instructors to customize the learning content and activities of an existing mini-course manually.
- **FR-17:** The platform shall allow instructors to use AI to customize the learning content and activities of an existing mini-course.
- **FR-18:** The platform shall allow instructors to generate a new mini-course for any subject or domain using AI by providing topics, requirements, and teaching content.
- **FR-19:** The platform shall allow instructors to review and refine AI-generated learning content and activities before using them in a mini-course.
- **FR-20:** The platform shall allow instructors to import students into their courses.
- **FR-21:** The platform shall allow instructors to view students' participation and engagement in course learning activities.

### 3.5 Learner and Course Workflows

- **FR-22:** The platform shall allow learners to browse available mini-courses and start a course.
- **FR-23:** The platform shall allow learners to view their progress and completion status for courses and activities.
- **FR-24:** The platform shall allow learners to save and revisit a self-directed learning path.
- **FR-25:** The platform shall allow instructors to save and publish a mini-course after reviewing its content.
- **FR-26:** The platform shall provide AI-supported learning assistance tailored to a learner's topic or learning goal.

## 4. Open Details

The requirements establish the expected capabilities but do not prescribe how learners are profiled, how AI-supported learning assistance works, what counts as course or activity completion, which student-import formats are supported, how participation and engagement are measured, or which course publication controls instructors need. These details should be defined during design and validation with learners and instructors.
