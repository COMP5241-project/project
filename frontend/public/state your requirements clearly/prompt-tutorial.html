Prompt precision lesson · 提示詞精準度

How one ambiguous phrase changes what an agent builds

An agent does not ask when a prompt is unclear — it guesses the most likely reading, silently, and builds from there. The guess is usually reasonable. It is just not always the one you intended.

當提示詞含糊時，agent 不會反問，而是「猜」一個最合理的解讀，然後照著做。這個猜測通常合理，但不一定是你想要的。

The original prompt · 原始提示詞
寫一個html程式，內容是一個動態svg：一個自行車騎著人往前走，然後後面有個鵜鶘拿鞭子抽他，人要有受擊反饋。

1 · The agent-guess map

One phrase, two whole scenes

The highlighted phrase is grammatically ambiguous. The agent had to pick a reading — and that single choice cascaded into a chain of downstream assumptions about who moves, what the whip hits, and what “受擊反饋” means. Click each reading to see the assumptions it forced.

The ambiguous phrase · 含糊的短句 一個自行車騎著人往前走

Who is riding whom? The sentence does not say. 誰騎著誰？句子沒有說明。

↓ the agent had to choose one of two readings ↓

This is the familiar, everyday reading — so it is the most likely guess.

  • ActorThe person is the one moving forward.
  • BicycleThe bicycle is the vehicle being ridden.
  • Whip hitsThe pelican whips the rider on the bike.
  • 受擊反饋The rider flinches — face swap, sweat, head jolt.

The literal reading of the clarified wording — a very different scene.

  • ActorThe bicycle is the one moving forward.
  • PersonThe person is underneath, being ridden on.
  • Whip hitsThe pelican whips the person under the bike.
  • 受擊反饋The person underneath flinches and struggles.

2 · Ambiguity taxonomy

The kinds of ambiguity to watch for

Most “the agent did the wrong thing” moments trace back to one of these. Each card shows how the type shows up in the whip prompt, and how to remove it. Click to expand.

In the whip prompt「自行車騎著人」— is the bike the actor, or the person? The verb “騎著” can attach to either.

How to remove itName the actor explicitly: “the bicycle rides on top of the person.”

In the whip prompt「拿鞭子抽他」— “他” (him) could be the rider or the person underneath, depending on the reading.

How to remove itReplace the pronoun with the exact target: “the whip strikes the person underneath.”

In the whip promptIs the person on the bike, or the bike on the person? The spatial relationship is the whole difference between the two demos.

How to remove itState the relationship: “the bicycle sits on the person’s back.”

In the whip prompt「動態svg」— how animated? A static image, a looping scroll, or a full scene with background motion?

How to remove itSet the scope: “a looping animated scene with a scrolling background.”

In the whip prompt“受擊反饋” (hit feedback) is assumed to mean a visible flinch — but it could be sound, color, or a shake.

How to remove itDefine it: “on each hit, the person flinches and a star burst appears.”

In the whip promptDoes the whip strike first and the flinch follow, or are they simultaneous? The timing changes the feel.

How to remove itOrder it: “the whip lands, then the person flinches within a fraction of a second.”

In the whip prompt「一個」(one) bicycle and person — but how many pelicans? One whip or several?

How to remove itCount them: “one pelican, one whip, one bicycle, one person.”

In the whip prompt「寫一個html程式」— one file? A project? With or without external libraries?

How to remove itSpecify: “a single self-contained HTML file, no external assets, runs by double-click.”

4 · Why this matters in COMP5241

Ambiguity is a requirements problem

This is not just about prompts — it is the same discipline the course teaches for working with AI. Clear, explicit requirements are what turn an agent’s guess into your intent.

Spec-driven development

Write the specification before the code. A precise prompt is a tiny spec: it states the what and why so the agent only has to decide the how.

“Bugs = missing requirements”

When the agent builds the wrong thing, the fix is usually to make the requirement explicit — not to patch the output. That permanent fix also helps the next agent.

Context engineering

Too little context and the agent guesses; too much and it is noisy. Curate exactly the detail that removes ambiguity — the same balance this tutorial practices.
