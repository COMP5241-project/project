Gold Rush Hook

The SDLC hook ’em up — DO’s are gold, DON’Ts are rocks.

Hook the green-labelled DO’s (requirements, code reviews, testing…) to earn marks.
Avoid the red-labelled DON’Ts (skipped tests, hardcoded keys…) — they cost marks and reel in slowly.
💎Diamonds and the heavy chest are rare big wins. Mystery bags can go either way.
🧨TNT deducts marks and blows nearby objects away. If you own Dynamite, press B to blast the heaviest rock for free.
🏆Hit each level’s target before the clock runs out, then spend marks in the camp shop.
Controls: Space / tap / click to drop the hook · B for dynamite · heavier objects reel in slower.

DO’s ✓ = gold · DON’Ts ✗ = rocks · an EduGenAI prototype built for the PolyU SE platform
